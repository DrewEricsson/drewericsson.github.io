# How to write a post

Hi Dad, this is how you put a post on your site. You don't need to touch any
code, it all happens in the browser.

## Writing a new post

1. Go to **app.pagescms.org** and log in with your GitHub account.
2. Pick the **WriteCraft** site from the list.
3. Click **Posts**, then **Add an entry**.
4. Fill in the boxes (explained below).
5. Click **Save**. The post will be on the site in a couple of minutes.

## The boxes

- **Title**: the title of the post, as it'll show on the page.
- **Date**: the day the post goes live. See "Scheduling" below.
- **Summary**: one sentence about the post. It shows on the blog page and in
  the preview when someone shares a link to it.
- **Cover image** (optional): a picture for the top of the post. Click the box
  to upload one.
- **Cover image description**: a sentence describing the picture, for people
  who can't see it. Fill this in whenever you add a cover image.
- **Tags** (optional): short labels like "science fiction" or "editing". Type
  one, press Enter, then type the next. Each tag becomes a link that shows
  every post with that tag.
- **Draft**: tick this to save a post without showing it on the site. Untick
  it when you're happy with it.
- **Body**: the post itself. More on that below.

## Writing the body

There's a small toolbar above the text:

- **Headings** for section titles.
- **Bold** and *italic* for emphasis.
- **Quotes** for anything you want set apart from the rest.
- **Images**: use the image button to put a picture in the text, then fill in
  its description.

A horizontal line turns into a ⁂ on the site. Use it where you'd normally
leave a gap between scenes in a story.

## Drafts

If you're not finished, tick **Draft** and save. Nobody will see it until you
untick the box and save again.

## Scheduling a post

To have a post appear on a certain day, set the **Date** to that day and save.
The site rebuilds itself every morning, so it'll show up on the day. If you
pick a date that's already passed, it appears straight away.

## Editing a post

1. Go to **Posts** and click the one you want to change.
2. Make your changes.
3. Click **Save**.

Changes go live in a couple of minutes.

## Deleting a post

Open the post and use the delete option in its menu. If you can't find it, ask
me first. Deleted posts can be a pain to get back.

## If something looks wrong

If a page looks broken, a picture's missing, or a change still hasn't shown up
after about ten minutes, don't try to fix it yourself. Message me with what you
were doing and what you saw, and send a screenshot if you can.
